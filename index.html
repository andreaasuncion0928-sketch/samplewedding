Our Story

It started with coffee.

We met over iced coffee on an ordinary afternoon in Manila. What was supposed to be a quick coffee date turned into hours of conversation, laughter, and the feeling that we had known each other much longer than we actually had.

Somehow, one coffee became another. Then another. And somewhere between the quiet afternoons, spontaneous plans, and everyday moments...

we became us.

What started as a simple coffee date became our favorite story to tell.

And now, we're getting married.
